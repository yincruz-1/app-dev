***Summary of the movie**
The story begins with Noah Calhoun, an elderly man living in a nursing home, who reads from a notebook to Allie Nelson, a woman suffering from Alzheimer's disease. The narrative flashes back to their youth in the 1930s when they fell deeply in love during a summer in New Bern, North Carolina. Despite their intense connection, societal pressures and family expectations force them apart. Noah writes to Allie every month, but her mother intercepts the letters, leading Allie to believe Noah has forgotten her. Years later, Allie, now engaged to a wealthy lawyer named Lon Hammond, sees a newspaper article about Noah's restoration of the old plantation house they once visited. This prompts her to return to New Bern, where she and Noah rekindle their romance over a few days, forcing Allie to confront her feelings for both men. The story explores themes of love, memory, and the enduring nature of true affection, culminating in Allie's decision to follow her heart.
